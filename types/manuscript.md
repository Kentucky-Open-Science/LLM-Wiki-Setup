# manuscript — writing you are producing

- **Tier**: catalog
- **Prefix**: `manuscript-`
- **Purpose**: one work of the user's own writing — a paper being written, a
  thesis chapter, a book, an essay, a grant narrative. The page tracks state:
  where the draft lives, its structure, its target, what is decided and what
  is open. (The endeavor around it may also be a `project-`; the manuscript
  page tracks the artifact.)

## Signals
- `*.tex`/`main.tex`, Overleaf syncs, `manuscript/`/`drafts/` dirs,
  Scrivener/Typora footprints, long-form markdown with front matter,
  `outline.md`
- conversation: "my chapter", "the submission", "draft two"

## Required sections
- `## Target` — venue/publisher/audience (link `venue-` if typed), format
  and length constraints, deadline
- `## State` — current draft version/location, section-by-section status
- `## Structure` — the outline as it currently stands
- `## Decisions` — settled questions (framing, scope, terminology) with
  dates, so they stop being relitigated
- `## Sources` — links to `paper-`/`reference-`/`interview-`-like pages it
  draws on

## Ask the user for
- The authoritative draft location (never guess among copies), the deadline,
  what is *settled* vs still open.

## Naming
- `manuscript-<slug>.md`, e.g. `manuscript-fieldnotes-book.md`.

## Typical relations
- Targets a `venue-`; draws on `paper-`/`reference-`; belongs to a
  `project-`; co-authors are `entity-` pages.
